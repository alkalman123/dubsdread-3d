How to play

Hitting a shot
  • Your caddie picks the club and shows the distance; pick a different chip to change it.
  • Aim with ◀ ▶ (or A / D), or tap the spot on the course you want to hit to.
  • Tap Swing (or press space) to start the power needle, again to set the power — the green mark is the caddie's number — and once more in the green band for a square face.
Putting
  • On the green you have the putter. The caddie gives the break in cups: aim that much to the side.
  • Tap Putt to start the needle and tap again on the green mark for the right pace. Past it is firm, short of it is soft.
Views and more
  • Shots are filmed like television: from behind as the ball climbs, then from beside where it lands.
  • View cycles cameras; Map shows the hole from above; Zones shows where shots like yours finish.
  • More has every hole, the tees, the weather, the sky and the graphics settings.
  • Your round is saved after every hole, so you can close the app and pick it up later.
