The Big Biner

Oversized wall-mount gear rack

$29.00

A carabiner-shaped panel, blown up to wall-art size, with a row of holes along the bottom to hang gear from and two mounting points up top. It also has "NOT FOR CLIMBING" printed directly into the plastic — because as much as it looks the part, it's a rack, not rated hardware.

ColorRock
  • Made to order — ships in 2–4 business days
  • Wall hardware not included — any two screws that fit the slots will do
  • Free standard shipping over $60
Not climbing protection The Big Biner is a decorative, load-light wall rack for hanging keys, gear loops and light hardware by hand — not a working carabiner. It says so right on the piece. Never clip it into a climbing system or trust it with body-weight loads.
Details & specs
Dimensions203 × 199 × 12.7 mm (8.0 × 7.8 × 0.5 in)
WeightApprox. 70–100 g depending on infill
MaterialPLA+, matte finish, visible layer lines
Hanging holesMore than a dozen, evenly spaced along the bottom arc
MountingOne round hole at the apex plus a slot on each arm — three points to screw to a wall or pegboard
Text"NOT FOR CLIMBING" embossed into the piece, permanently
Mounting

Three mounting points: a round hole at the top apex and a slotted hole on each arm. Use screws sized for your wall (drywall anchors recommended for anything heavier than a couple of keychains) through all three points for the most stable hang.

Material & care

PLA+, the same material as the rest of the Crux lineup. Wipe clean with a dry or slightly damp cloth, keep it indoors, and out of prolonged direct heat (PLA+ softens above roughly 50–60°C / 122–140°F).

Made to order, 2–4 business days before it ships. Full policy on the shipping & returns page.
