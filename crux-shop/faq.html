FAQ

Questions, answered.

Ordering & material

What is the Rock Ring made of?

PLA+, a durable plant-based bioplastic. It's FDM 3D-printed, so you'll see fine layer lines — that's the nature of the process, not a flaw.

How long does an order take?

Everything is printed to order, so plan on 2–4 business days before it ships, then your chosen shipping speed on top of that.

Do colorways ever run out?

Rarely — since nothing is pulled from a shelf of pre-made stock, a colorway is only unavailable if we're out of that specific filament, which we restock quickly.

Can I get a custom color?

Ask us — for larger or custom-color orders we're generally happy to see what's possible.

Sizing & use

How big is it?

127 × 114 × 64 mm (5.0 × 4.5 × 2.5 in), about 180g. Full breakdown on the product page.

Does it mount to a wall?

No — it's freestanding by design, with a flat base that sits on a desk, shelf or van console. There's no wall hardware included or required.

Is it climbing-safety equipment?

No. The Rock Ring is a storage accessory, not rated climbing gear. Never use it as an anchor, belay point, or any load-bearing part of a climbing system.

What can I put through the ports?

Anything that fits through a ~90×27mm or ~60×24mm opening — a chalk bag drawstring, a keyring, folded sunglasses, a watch. It's not sized for racking hardware like quickdraws or cams.

Care

How do I clean it?

A dry or slightly damp cloth. Skip the dishwasher and anything abrasive.

Will it warp in the sun or a hot car?

PLA+ starts to soften around 50–60°C (122–140°F), which a car dashboard in direct summer sun can exceed. Keep it indoors or out of prolonged direct heat.

Shipping & returns

Where do you ship?

The US and Canada at checkout; other countries can reach out and we'll quote a rate.

What's your return policy?

30 days, unused and undamaged. Full policy on the shipping & returns page.

Mine arrived damaged — now what?

Email us a photo and your order number — we'll get a replacement into the queue.
