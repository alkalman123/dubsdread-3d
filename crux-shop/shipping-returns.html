Shipping & returns

Processing time Shipping rates International Returns Damaged or lost

Processing time

Nothing on Crux sits pre-made on a shelf — every order is sliced and printed after it comes in. Plan on 2–4 business days of print & pack time before your order ships. During a busy stretch (a restock, a holiday) that can run a little longer; we'll say so at checkout or by email if it does.

Shipping rates

  • Standard — $5.95, 3–6 business days after processing. Free on orders over $60.
  • Expedited — $14.95, 2 business days after processing.

Rates are calculated at checkout and cover the US and Canada by default.

International

Outside the US and Canada, contact us before ordering and we'll quote a shipping rate and realistic timeline. Import duties or taxes, if your country charges them, are the buyer's responsibility.

Returns

If a Rock Ring isn't right for you, return it within 30 days of delivery for a refund, provided it's unused and undamaged. To start a return, email us your order number — we'll confirm an address and next steps.

  • Buyer covers return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or wrong
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the return is received
  • Custom-color or bulk orders are handled case-by-case — ask before you order if that matters to you

Damaged or lost in transit

3D-printed parts can chip if a box gets thrown around. If something arrives damaged, or never arrives at all, email us a photo (for damage) and your order number — we'll sort a replacement or a refund, no return shipping required on our error.
