The flagship

Meet the Rock Ring.

One solid block, two open channels. Thread a drawstring, a keyring or a folded pair of sunglasses through and it stops sliding off the bench, the dashboard or the top of the crash pad. No wall, no hardware, no drilling — it just sits.

  • Two open ports: a wide lower channel, a narrower upper one
  • Freestanding — flat base, no mounting required
  • Printed in durable PLA+, six colorways

Material & specs

Printed, not molded.

Every Rock Ring is FDM-printed in premium PLA+ — a durable, plant-based bioplastic with a soft matte finish. Layer lines are part of the look, not a defect: it's a 3D-printed object and we'd rather you know that going in.

Dimensions127 × 114 × 64 mm (5.0 × 4.5 × 2.5 in), H×W×D
WeightApprox. 180 g (0.4 lb), varies slightly by color/infill
