Why does this ship separately?

The 3D-printed gear is sliced and printed by hand to order; this tee is produced and shipped by a separate print-on-demand partner so it can go out fast without waiting on a print queue. If your order includes both, expect two packages.

Sizing & fit

Unisex, true-to-size fit. Between sizes or unsure? Ask us before ordering — exchanges on a misprinted size are easy, exchanges on a correctly-printed "wrong choice" size are not.
