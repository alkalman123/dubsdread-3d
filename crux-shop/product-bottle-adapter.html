Hydro Flask ↔ Nalgene Adapter

Split-ring bottle cap adapter

$14.00

A split-ring collar that lets a Hydro Flask-style cap thread onto a Nalgene-style wide-mouth bottle. The ring is open on one side and cinches down with a small screw across the gap, so it clamps to the bottle rather than relying on threads alone.

ColorInk
Check your bottle before ordering Nalgene and Hydro Flask have both changed thread and neck dimensions across product generations. This adapter is sized to the common wide-mouth profiles from both brands, but we can't guarantee fit sight-unseen — measure your bottle's neck and cap, or ask us first if you're not sure.

Fit & compatibility

This is sized around the common Nalgene wide-mouth and Hydro Flask standard profiles, but neither brand's dimensions are perfectly consistent across their product history. If you've got calipers, measuring your bottle's neck OD and your cap's ID before ordering is the surest way to avoid a return.
