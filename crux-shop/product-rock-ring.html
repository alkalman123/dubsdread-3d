The Rock Ring

Freestanding gear valet

$34.00

A dome-topped block that sits on its own base — no wall, no hardware. Two open ports run straight through it so a chalk bag drawstring, a keyring, a folded pair of sunglasses or a watch have somewhere to live besides the bottom of your pack.

Not climbing protection The Rock Ring is a gear-storage accessory for your bench, desk or van — it is not rated climbing equipment. Never use it as an anchor, belay point, or any other load-bearing part of a climbing system.

Material & care

Printed in PLA+, a plant-based bioplastic that's tougher and less brittle than standard PLA. It keeps its shape well at room temperature but will soften above roughly 50–60°C (122–140°F) — so it's happy on a desk or gear bench, less happy on a hot car dashboard in July.

  • Wipe clean with a dry or slightly damp cloth
  • Indoor use only
  • Keep out of prolonged direct summer sun or hot vehicle interiors
  • Not dishwasher or microwave safe
Shipping & returns

Every Rock Ring is printed to order, so please allow 2–4 business days before it ships. Standard shipping runs 3–6 business days after that (free over $60); expedited shipping is available at checkout.

Not the right fit? Return an unused, undamaged print within 30 days for a refund. Full details on the shipping & returns page.

Is this made for climbing gear specifically?

It's sized and shaped for the small stuff that piles up around a climbing habit — a chalk bag's drawstring, a keyring, folded sunglasses, a watch — not for racking hardware like quickdraws or cams. Think gear-bench valet, not a gear loop.
