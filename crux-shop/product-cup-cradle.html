The Cup Cradle

Nalgene-to-cupholder adapter

$16.00

A slim stem sized for a standard car console cupholder steps up into a wide, scalloped basket that cradles a full-size wide-mouth Nalgene so it rides upright instead of sliding around on the next turn. One side of the rim is scooped low — enough to get a finger under the bottle or let its carry loop hang free instead of getting pinned against the plastic.

Fit & compatibility

Most car console cupholders run 2.5–3.5 in (64–89 mm); the stem here is sized toward the middle of that range at roughly 70 mm. Deep, narrow, or oddly-stepped cupholders (some SUVs and minivans use non-round or multi-tier wells) may not seat the same way — check your cupholder's diameter before ordering if it looks unusual.

In use

Steady in the cupholder, all the way to the trailhead.

The stem drops into the console cupholder like any other cup; the flared basket picks up above the console rim and cradles the wider body of a wide-mouth Nalgene so it can't tip on a turn or rattle around on washboard road. The scalloped notch keeps the bottle's carry loop free to grab on the way out.
