Not climbing protection The Gatekeeper is a decorative, load-light gear organizer for hanging a trad rack, slings, a helmet and other gear by hand — not a working carabiner. It says so right on the piece. Never clip it into a climbing system or trust it with body-weight loads.

Hanging it up

The single hole at the top apex is enough to hang the whole board from one hook, bolt or nail — a garage stud, a van's tie-down point, a gear-closet rail. Use the slotted holes on each arm too if you want it flush against a wall (drywall anchors recommended for anything past a light load). Then load the bottom arc: cams and draws clip through the round holes, a helmet's chin strap or brim loop works through the wider ones.

Material & care

PLA+, the same material as the rest of the Rackhouse lineup. Wipe clean with a dry or slightly damp cloth, keep it indoors, and out of prolonged direct heat (PLA+ softens above roughly 50–60°C / 122–140°F).

In use

A full rack and a helmet, off one hook.

Hang it from a single garage hook, van tie-down or gear-closet rail through the top hole. Cams and draws clip through the round holes along the bottom arc; a helmet's chin strap or brim loop works through the wider ones. Everything stays visible and off the floor instead of buried in a duffel.
